# Getting started

This page is for the person who writes the notes. It takes you from the first
visit to your first saved note.

## The first visit

Open the address of your installation. Because no account exists yet, the page
says **Create your account**. Enter an email address and a password of at least
12 characters, and type the password again to confirm it. Select **Create
account**.

That account is the only account. It owns every note. There is no second
account, no invitation, and no sharing.

Every later visit shows **Sign in** instead. Enter the same address and
password.

There is no "forgot password" link in the application. If you lose the
password, the person who runs the server recovers the account with a command on
the server. See [Backup and restore](backup-and-restore.md).

## What you see first

Your new account already holds one note, **Welcome to NotesApp**. It is a
normal note: you can change it, rename it, or delete it.

The screen has three parts.

- The **header** says where you are: your workspace, the folders above the
  note, and the name of the note or the page. Then the actions of that page,
  then **Search** and your account.
- The **sidebar** holds your workspace, **New note**, **New folder**, the whole
  note tree under **All notes**, and at the bottom a line that says where your
  saves are, plus **Trash**.
- The rest of the screen is the note you have open.

On a phone the sidebar is hidden. Select the menu button in the header to open
it, and **Escape** or a tap outside it to close it.

## Your first note

1. Select **New note** in the sidebar. The note opens at once and its name is
   selected, so you can type the name over it.
2. Press **Tab** or select the text area, and start writing. Markdown works
   here. See [Markdown](markdown.md).
3. Press **Ctrl + S**, or **Cmd + S** on a Mac, or select the **Save** button.

The Save button tells you where your work is. It reads **Saved on this device**
while the version waits, and **Saved** when your server has it. Typing alone
never makes a version, so nothing reaches your server until you select Save.
See [Saving and history](saving-and-history.md).

## Where to go next

- Organize your notes with [Notes and folders](notes-and-folders.md).
- Press **Ctrl + P** or **Cmd + P** to open the command palette, which searches
  your notes and runs the common actions.
- Choose a theme in [Appearance and shortcuts](appearance-and-shortcuts.md).
