# The audit of 2026-10-02

Status: active

The [GitHub project board](https://github.com/users/bkhanale/projects/2) owns
priority and release assignment. This plan closes every issue that the audit
of 2026-10-02 opened: the
[product audit](../audits/2026-10-02/product-audit.md) and the
[performance audit](../audits/2026-10-02/performance.md). Issues 233 to 267
are its rows.

## Decisions

These records govern the work. A record that the work adds is listed under
its group when it lands.

- [0001](../decisions/0001-one-markdown-grammar.md): the editor grammar and `packages/markdown` stay one language.
- [0004](../decisions/0004-standard-links-between-notes.md): a link between notes is a standard link.
- [0022](../decisions/0022-autosave-and-automatic-versions.md): a pause, leaving the note or Ctrl + S makes a version.
- [0060](../decisions/0060-the-published-page-takes-every-style.md): the published page takes every emitted style.
- [0136](../decisions/0136-the-initial-javascript-budget-follows-the-inequality-gap.md): the budget of the first load.

## Item notes

- A group that makes an architectural choice writes its design and records
  before its code.
- Every changed screen gets a screenshot in light and in dark.
